About

Systems thinking, applied to AI.

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at VIT Chennai (AI & Robotics specialization, CGPA 8.8), concurrently pursuing a BS in Data Science and Programming from IIT Madras. My work sits at the intersection of applied AI and systems engineering — I don't just call an LLM API, I design the pipeline around it: what a cheap regex layer should catch, what needs retrieval, and what actually needs a model's judgment.

That thinking shaped my flagship project, an AI RFQ compliance verifier built on a 3-layer evaluation pipeline instead of a single brittle prompt.
